# Quarterly Operations Report — Q3 2026 (Sample)

## Executive summary
Support ticket volume rose 14% quarter over quarter while median first-response time improved from 3.9 to 3.1 hours. The volume increase is concentrated in one product area (billing), which accounts for 61% of the growth.

## Method
Data were drawn from the support platform for July 1 – September 30, 2026, and compared with Q2 2026. Tickets were grouped by product area and priority. Duplicate and test tickets were excluded (n = 212).

## Findings
| Measure | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total tickets | 8,412 | 9,590 | +14% |
| Median first response | 3.9 hrs | 3.1 hrs | −21% |
| Billing share of tickets | 22% | 31% | +9 pts |
| Escalations to engineering | 118 | 121 | +3% |

1. Response time improved despite higher volume, following the March staffing change.
2. Billing tickets grew after the June pricing-page change; the top three billing questions are all answered in existing help content that customers are not finding.
3. Escalation volume is flat, indicating the growth is in routine questions, not product defects.

## Recommendations
1. Add the three top billing answers to the pricing page itself, not only the help center.
2. Review billing ticket trends again in Q4 before adding headcount.
3. Keep the current first-response staffing model; it absorbed a 14% increase with room to spare.

## Limitations
Ticket categories are assigned by the first responder and were not re-audited for this report; small miscategorizations are possible but would not change the direction of any finding above.
