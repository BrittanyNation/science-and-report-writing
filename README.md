# Science & Report Writing Samples

Two samples showing the two directions of technical communication: making research readable for a general audience, and making operational data decision-ready for professionals.

## Skills demonstrated
- Plain-language science explanation (accurate, no hype, jargon translated)
- Technical report structure: summary, method, findings, recommendations
- Presenting data in tables a reader can act on
- Separating what the data shows from what it does not show

## Contents
- `samples/science-explainer-sleep.md` — a study explained for general readers
- `samples/technical-report-q3-operations.md` — a quarterly operations report
